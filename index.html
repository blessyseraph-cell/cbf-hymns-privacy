Privacy Policy – CBF HYMNS

Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. Introduction

CBF HYMNS is a Christian hymns application that provides Telugu Christian hymns, lyrics and English transliterations. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

2. Information We Collect

CBF HYMNS does not collect, request, or transmit users' personal information. The app does not require an account or personal details such as name, email address, phone number or location.

3. Local Data Storage

App preferences, including bookmarked or favourite hymns, may be stored locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to our servers.

4. Advertising and Analytics

CBF HYMNS does not display advertisements or use analytics or tracking services.

5. Children's Privacy

CBF HYMNS does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. No personal information is required to access the app's content.

6. Third-Party Services

CBF HYMNS does not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the developer using the contact email provided on the CBF HYMNS Google Play Store listing.
